A little note from Katie

From one mom to another—
truly, don’t worry.

I know exactly what it takes to get everyone dressed, fed, out the door and settled enough to arrive. I also know kids may need a snack, a cuddle, a minute to warm up—or a good run around.

None of that means your session is going wrong. I’ll meet your family exactly where you are, follow your children’s lead and keep things light and playful. The beautiful moments always find us, and I’ll do everything I can to make the experience feel easy and genuinely enjoyable for the kiddos, too.
